# Dark GT stage diagnosis

The fixed dark population has 46 GTs; 22 are uncovered by the saved default. All 22/22 have zero actual red/orange support pixels. Native-resolution standard-HSV support is also zero in 22/22; resizing or denoising did not remove a previously qualifying native pixel in this cohort. Both default and dark enhancement produce zero GT-derived anchors and zero GT intersection at every box stage for these cases. In the denoised working image, 20 GT rectangles have no hue-band pixels; 2 contain hue-band pixels but none meet even S>=0.25. Hue in low-saturation/dark pixels is unstable, and this does not prove the real object lacks red/orange surfaces. The failures are 1 source-label type(s) across only 2 images, not independent scenes. No covering ROI was lost to window size, area rejection, green/blue rejection, or geometric merging for these particular failures: no support-derived covering ROI existed upstream. Larger windows cannot create an anchor from zero color support; an unrelated nearby anchor could still cover a GT incidentally. Review the two source frames/GT visibility and color definition before further window-size tuning. Do not claim these observations generalize to all dark scenes or that the supplied labels are verified.

Before masking there are no proposals, so ROI coverage is undefined. Mask pixel fraction and anchor-envelope coverage are different diagnostics, not interchangeable with ROI coverage.
