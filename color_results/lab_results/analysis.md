# Lab histogram matching after ROI refinement

Lab histograms (lab_histogram, bins [8, 16, 16]) were extracted on every FINAL window after V4 coarse ROI generation, on the same 12 training images and 73 canonical GT boxes as ROI_refinement. ROI windows cover 34/73 GT at >=95% (V4 ceiling 36). Matching with D3 against class-mean references and validation thresholds (cone 1.812, roadworks 1.307, vertical_pannel 1.845) accepts 7,984 of 30,159 windows per image (26.5%); accepted windows still cover 33 GT at >=95% (1 lost by matching), and 26 GT have a same-class accepted window with IoU >= 0.5. After same-class NMS at IoU 0.5: 3,938.4 detections per image, TP 16, FP 47245, FN 57; precision 0.000, recall 0.219, F1 0.001. Per class: cone: P 0.000 R 0.286 (TP 2, FP 11265, FN 5); roadworks: P 0.000 R 0.000 (TP 0, FP 2390, FN 3); vertical_pannel: P 0.000 R 0.222 (TP 14, FP 33590, FN 49). Unmatched-prediction reasons: background 43470, localization 3766, wrong_class 6, duplicate 3. References: 300 training-split images (1483 GT boxes); thresholds: 120 validation-split images (6531 regions, 5950 background windows). None of the 12 evaluation images was used for fitting, but they are official-training images, so this is development evidence, not held-out test performance. Background calibration windows come from unannotated regions, and the GT is not exhaustive.
