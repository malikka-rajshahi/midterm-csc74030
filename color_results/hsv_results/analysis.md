# HSV histogram matching after ROI refinement

HSV histograms (hsv_histogram, bins [18, 8, 8]) were extracted on every FINAL window after V4 coarse ROI generation, on the same 12 training images and 73 canonical GT boxes as ROI_refinement. ROI windows cover 34/73 GT at >=95% (V4 ceiling 36). Matching with D3 against class-mean references and validation thresholds (cone 3.025, roadworks 1.668, vertical_pannel 3.264) accepts 4,210 of 30,159 windows per image (14.0%); accepted windows still cover 34 GT at >=95% (0 lost by matching), and 26 GT have a same-class accepted window with IoU >= 0.5. After same-class NMS at IoU 0.5: 2,067.0 detections per image, TP 19, FP 24785, FN 54; precision 0.001, recall 0.260, F1 0.002. Per class: cone: P 0.000 R 0.286 (TP 2, FP 11054, FN 5); roadworks: P 0.000 R 0.000 (TP 0, FP 788, FN 3); vertical_pannel: P 0.001 R 0.270 (TP 17, FP 12943, FN 46). Unmatched-prediction reasons: background 21458, localization 3322, wrong_class 5. References: 300 training-split images (1483 GT boxes); thresholds: 120 validation-split images (6531 regions, 5950 background windows). None of the 12 evaluation images was used for fitting, but they are official-training images, so this is development evidence, not held-out test performance. Background calibration windows come from unannotated regions, and the GT is not exhaustive.
